# Product

## Register

product

## Users

TutorSpace is used by clients who need to find a tutor, tutors who manage requests and students, and moderators or admins who keep marketplace quality under control. Most sessions are task-focused: compare tutors, send a request, answer a client, update a schedule, review complaints, or inspect platform health.

## Product Purpose

TutorSpace connects students and parents with tutors, then gives tutors lightweight CRM tools for requests, schedules, students, messages, and subscriptions. The interface should make education logistics feel organized, calm, and trustworthy.

## Brand Personality

Warm, composed, capable. The product should feel human and educational, but not childish; professional, but not bureaucratic.

## Anti-references

Avoid loud startup gradients, casino-like marketplace urgency, cold enterprise dashboards, decorative glassmorphism, and payment language that implies TutorSpace is a guarantor for lesson payments.

## Design Principles

1. Calm is a feature: every screen should reduce anxiety and make the next action obvious.
2. The task stays central: visual personality supports catalog, CRM, moderation, and admin work without competing with it.
3. Trust through clarity: prices, statuses, complaints, subscription states, and profile details should be legible at a glance.
4. Warm professionalism: use soft tactile surfaces, restrained accent color, and readable Russian typography.
5. One product language: public pages, workspaces, and admin screens should share the same component vocabulary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, and layouts that remain usable on laptop and mobile widths. Do not rely on color alone for status communication.
